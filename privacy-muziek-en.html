5. Rights to AI-Generated Music

IMPORTANT: This section describes rights relating to AI-generated music. It is important that you understand these terms before using the service.

5.1 Ownership and Usage Rights

Rights to AI-generated music are governed as follows:

  • Your input: The input you provide (such as themes, moods, or topics) constitutes only a small part of the creative process. This input serves as inspiration and guidance, but the final musical work is largely generated by AI and curated by us.
  • Ownership of the music: Ownership of the generated music rests with Zonder Stropdas Company BV, under Suno Pro license terms. You receive a usage license for the music delivered to you, not a transfer of ownership.
  • Usage license: Upon delivery of the music, you receive a personal, non-exclusive license to use the music for the agreed purpose. The exact usage rights are specified in the agreement or invoice.

5.2 What You May and May Not Do

Permitted Not Permitted
Use for the agreed purpose (e.g., personal, event, video) Reselling or sublicensing the music to third parties
Private listening and sharing with immediate circle Claiming copyright or ownership of the music
Use on social media (if agreed upon) Registering with music rights organizations (PROs)
Modifying for personal use (if agreed upon) Commercial exploitation without explicit permission

5.3 Suno Pro License

We use a Suno Pro subscription, which enables commercial use of the generated music. This means:

  • The music may be used commercially within the terms of your usage license
  • Suno's Pro terms allow monetization, but this right is managed by us
  • For extended commercial rights (e.g., advertising, film, streaming), additional arrangements are required

Consult the Suno Terms of Service for more details about the underlying license.

5.4 No Traditional Copyright

AI-generated content does not qualify for traditional copyright protection in many jurisdictions, as copyright requires human creativity. The legal status of AI-generated works is still evolving. This means:

  • Neither you nor we can claim full copyright protection on purely AI-generated elements
  • Protection lies primarily in the contractual agreements between you and us
  • We advise against registering the music with copyright organizations

5.5 Our Creative Process

The value of our service lies in:

  • Professional curation: We generate multiple variants and carefully select the best results
  • Quality control: Each track is evaluated for musical quality and suitability
  • Alignment: We ensure the music matches your wishes and intended use
  • Expertise: Our knowledge of AI music generation ensures optimal results
